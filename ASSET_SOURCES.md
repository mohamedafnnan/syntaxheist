# Visual asset sources

The brief does not require photography or logos, but the finished site uses two supporting FISAT assets so the fictional event is grounded in its real venue and organizer.

- `public/assets/fisat-ieee-student-branch.jpg` — IEEE FISAT Student Branch wordmark found through image search from the IEEE FISAT Student Branch LinkedIn presence: https://www.linkedin.com/company/ieee-fisat-student-branch/
- `public/assets/fisat-campus.jpg` — FISAT campus image found through image search and associated with the official FISAT Campus Life page: https://fisat.ac.in/campus-life/

Venue copy was checked against the official FISAT Campus Life page. It identifies the campus as Hormis Nagar, Mookkannoor, on the outskirts of Angamaly, and gives the official postal address used on the site: Federal Institute of Science And Technology (FISAT), Hormis Nagar, Mookkannoor P O, Angamaly, Ernakulam Dt., Kerala, India, Pin - 683 577.

These files are kept locally in the repository instead of relying on hotlinked third-party images at runtime.
